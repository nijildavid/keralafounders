# Finland open questions (agent draft, unreviewed; not legal advice)

## Judgment calls made while drafting
1. **Country-specific section.** I added one extra section, `residence-trap`, because Finland's PRH rule (permit needed when no EEA-resident board member or managing director) and the Finnish personal identity code requirement are the most likely blockers for a founder arriving from outside the EU. Remove it if you prefer the lean structure.
2. **Dropped the notary column content.** Finland has no notary step in PRH's process; I wrote "no notary step in PRH's process" instead of "no", because the pages read do not state it as a rule.
3. **Liability wording.** PRH pages read do not spell out unlimited liability for a toiminimi or limited liability for an Oy. I wrote these as editorial and labelled them so in the table.
4. **Processing time.** I quoted PRH's own March 2026 "about a week" and described the 12 Oct 2026 status table honestly as arrival dates under processing, rather than inventing a number.
5. **next_check_due 2027-01-15.** YEL contributions and income limits, PRH fees and Vero rates tend to reset on 1 January; the proposed corporate tax cut (20% to 18%) would also matter from tax year 2027.
6. **Visa section drafted without Migri's own pages.** migri.fi returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching (I did not try to bypass it). I used Migri's Enter Finland e-service pages, Suomi.fi, the ELY Centre and Business Finland. Fees and processing times are absent, on purpose.
7. **Held claim in the taxes section.** General B2B e-invoicing mandate: not confirmed, so it is a `hold` claim.

## Conflicts between sources
- Vero's English "VAT for small business" page states the EUR 20,000 threshold but contains an example mentioning a 2024 threshold of EUR 22,000. The Finnish-language Vero page (18 May 2026) says EUR 20,000. I used EUR 20,000 (2026). A local professional should confirm the example is only an old illustration.
- ETK's age wording (insured 18 to 69, but cover ends at 68, 69 or 70 by birth year) is internally inconsistent; the draft repeats it and tells readers to check their own birth year.
- PRH's older figures (EUR 370 and 380 for paper or no-ID filings) appeared in search summaries; PRH's 2026 fee page (updated 1 Jan 2026) shows EUR 400 and EUR 300, which I used. I did not open the older pages.
