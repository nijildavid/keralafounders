# Slovakia: open questions (agent draft, unreviewed)

Agent review, not legal advice. Nothing here has been reviewed by a lawyer.

## Judgment calls I made
1. **Minimum capital is EUR 5,000, not EUR 1.** One secondary page (podnikajte.sk, written Oct 2014) says capital would fall to EUR 1 from 2015. The consolidated Commercial Code in force from 17 Aug 2026 (SK1, section 108(1)) still says EUR 5,000, so I used the statute. I did not verify the separate j.s.a. (simple company on shares) form and left it out.
2. **Trade fee EUR 7 / EUR 22**, not EUR 5 / EUR 15. Search snippets quoted EUR 5 and 15 (older amounts). The consolidated fee schedule (SK3) in force 1 Sep 2026 says EUR 7 and 22. The statute won.
3. **Residence-permit fee EUR 330 / 200**, not EUR 232 / 132.50, which appear in an old amending article inside Act 404/2011. Used the consolidated fee schedule.
4. **Two country-specific sections**: the trade-types section (carries the non-OECD register-entry trap) and the flat-rate expense section. The Standard allows 0 to 2.
5. **next_check_due 2027-01-08**: e-invoicing becomes mandatory 1 Jan 2027 and rates, thresholds and the subsistence minimum reset on 1 Jan or 1 Jul.
6. **Dates**: all sources were read on 2026-10-12, the date used throughout, even though the PR was finished a day later.
7. **Quick-fact "gotcha"** uses social insurance, not the permit, because the permit content is held.
8. **Director residence**: I wrote that the Commercial Code sets no nationality or residence condition. That is an inference from reading section 133(2) only; the register law or other acts could add conditions.

## Conflicts between sources
- The Sociálna poisťovňa page (SK9) states the no-obligation income limit as EUR 2,876.90 and also uses EUR 2,983.37 in an example (10.5 times 284.13). It also uses EUR 9,144 and EUR 9,720 for the upper band in different places. Held.
- Search snippets disagreed on whether an electronic trade-licence filing is discounted or free. The fee table does not settle it. Held.

## A local professional must confirm
- Whether an s.r.o. needs a Slovak registered-office address with proof of right to use the premises, and any bank-account practices for non-residents.
- Notary or lawyer fees for the founding deed (not verified).
- How a managing director is insured for social security, and the health cover of a non-resident director (SK8 section 3(9) reads as no Slovak public cover from that role alone).
- Whether the six-month holiday on social insurance has any health-insurance equivalent. I found none in the health act text but did not read it fully.
- Whether eKasa applies to the founder's actual sales channel.
- Annual tax-return deadlines, VAT return frequency and VAT status of non-resident-owned companies (not read).
- The e-invoicing law number quoted in secondary results (385/2025) was not checked; I cited only the Financial Directorate FAQ.

## Could not verify
- Everything in the visa section beyond the statute and the July 2024 FAQ: current consulate practice, real processing time (the FAQ gives a 90-day legal period), the income amount for renewal, and the effect of the OECD distinction on Indian nationals (India is not an OECD member, so the register-entry route applies on the FAQ's wording).
- Any second-agent re-verification (Standard QA check 4).
