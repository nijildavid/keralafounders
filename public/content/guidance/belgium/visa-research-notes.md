# Belgium — non-EU founder / professional card route: research notes (2026-09-25)

Not part of the published guide — `belgium/guide.json` deliberately omits any
visa/immigration section per the task instructions. These are working notes
for a future pass, based only on pages actually fetched in this session.

## The route

Non-Belgian, non-EU/EEA/Swiss nationals who want to be self-employed
(sole proprietor or company founder/director) in Belgium need a
**professional card** ("beroepskaart" / "carte professionnelle"). EU/EEA and
Swiss nationals do not need one and simply register with their local
municipality. (Confirmed on business.belgium.be's general "Requirements for
starting a business" page — fetched directly.)

## Administration is regional, not federal

The professional card is administered by each **region** separately —
Flanders, Brussels-Capital, and (not directly checked this session) Wallonia
each run their own process. This is a genuine federal/regional split worth
flagging for the future guide, similar to how KBO registration is federal
but professional-competency rules are regional.

### Flanders (Vlaanderen) — vlaanderen.be, fetched directly
- Foreign nationals need a professional card to pursue self-employed
  activity in the Flemish Region; certain categories are exempt (page did
  not enumerate which, beyond referring to a separate "exemptions" page not
  fetched).
- Flanders offers expedited processing via an "Innovation & Startup Permit"
  track for founders who get a signed letter confirming they are being
  mentored by / collaborating with a Flemish innovator, incubator, or
  knowledge institution.
- Fees and validity period were **not** stated on the page fetched — it
  referred applicants to a separate simulator and to "Team beroepskaarten"
  (phone: +32 2 553 43 00, weekdays 9:00–17:00) for specifics.

### Brussels-Capital Region — economy-employment.brussels, fetched directly
- Exempt from needing a card: EEA/Swiss nationals, holders of a permanent
  CIRE residence certificate, recognised refugees, spouses/partners
  assisting a self-employed person, and some short-term categories
  (business travellers, lecturers, journalists, athletes, artists under
  specific conditions).
- Three conditions to qualify: (1) a right of residence in place before or
  at the same time as the card application, (2) meeting all legal/regulatory
  obligations for the intended activity, and (3) the project must
  demonstrably benefit the Brussels-Capital Region (innovation, job
  creation, improved services, or cultural contribution).
- Where to apply: at a Belgian consulate/embassy if applying from abroad, or
  at a recognised business counter if already resident in Belgium with a
  valid "model A registration certificate".
- Required documents include a completed application form, a criminal
  record extract, an ID copy, two ID photos, and a business plan
  (**max 20 pages**).
- **Fees**: EUR 140 payable on submission (at the counter or the
  diplomatic post), and a further EUR 90 when the business counter actually
  issues the card.
- **Validity**: up to 5 years maximum; an initial card is typically granted
  for a 2-year probationary period.
- **Processing time**: about 3 months for a complete application.
