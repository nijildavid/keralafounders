About

Mapping Kerala's business ecosystem across Europe.

Kerala Founders is an independent directory documenting Kerala-connected businesses, founders and professionals across Europe.

The Kerala business ecosystem extends far beyond startups. It includes technology companies, restaurants, healthcare practices, construction businesses, logistics, retail, professional services and many other businesses built by people with roots in Kerala.

The goal is simple: make this ecosystem easier to discover, connect and understand.

Research & verification

Businesses are researched across company websites, professional networks, business directories, community platforms, social media and local sources. Information is cross-checked wherever possible, with records classified as:

  • Verified — supported by credible evidence
  • Partially verified — some information remains unconfirmed
  • Unverified — a Kerala connection has not yet been sufficiently established

A business may qualify through its founders, owners, Kerala/Malayali identity or meaningful connection to the Kerala community.

An evolving map

Kerala Founders is an ongoing effort to document the people and businesses shaping the Kerala-connected ecosystem across Europe.
