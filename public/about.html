Making the Kerala–EU founder ecosystem visible.

Kerala Founders is a directory of Keralite founders and companies building across the European Union.

We want to make these people and companies easier to discover, connect and learn from — across technology, AI, healthcare, food, manufacturing, finance and beyond.

The directory is designed around a simple idea: different cities, shared roots.
